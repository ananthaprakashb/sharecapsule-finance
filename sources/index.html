Financial Health benchmark sources

The current peer benchmark uses values transcribed and reviewed from the BLS Consumer Expenditures in 2023 report: national income-quintile means and Census-region means. ShareCapsule regionalizes the income-quintile values using the region-to-U.S. ratio for each category.

The resulting values are a ShareCapsule estimate derived from BLS inputs. They are not the official BLS region-by-income cross-tabulated means. The benchmark file carries an explicit isExactCrossTab: false marker, source year and calculation method, and automated publishing checks reject a mislabeled dataset.

Source-quality hierarchy

PrioritySource typeHow we use it
1Law, regulator, agency or official programRules, limits, definitions, deadlines, consumer protections, official guidance and public statistical inputs.
2Original research or recognized standards bodyEvidence, technical standards and methodology where primary public-agency guidance is not the right source.
3Qualified professional or major institution educationContext and practical explanation, checked against primary material when the topic is regulated or time-sensitive.
4Secondary articles and commentaryDiscovery and perspective, not the sole authority for a material rule, numeric limit or public benchmark.

How sources appear on guide pages

Each specialist guide includes an “Authoritative resources” section when primary references are directly relevant. Time-sensitive numerical values should state the applicable year. If ShareCapsule makes a planning judgment that is not an official rule, it should appear in a labeled “ShareCapsule opinion” block.

A link to an external source does not imply that the source endorses ShareCapsule Finance. Likewise, ShareCapsule’s interpretation or calculation should not be treated as the source’s official wording or estimate.

Correction policy

Prefer correction over silent ambiguity

If a source changes or a page is materially outdated, the page should be updated and its “Last reviewed” date refreshed.

Do not preserve stale numeric limits for SEO

Annual tax, retirement, insurance, benchmark and similar values should be updated or clearly labeled with their year.

Separate source facts from recommendations

Official facts should remain attributable; ShareCapsule estimates and heuristics should remain visibly labeled as our calculation or planning opinion.
