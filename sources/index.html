Sources

Primary sources first for rules, limits and consumer guidance.

Financial information changes. ShareCapsule Finance prefers authoritative first-party sources for statements about laws, contribution limits, consumer protections, official definitions and government guidance.

Source-quality hierarchy

PrioritySource typeHow we use it
1Law, regulator, agency or official programRules, limits, definitions, deadlines, consumer protections and official guidance.
2Original research or recognized standards bodyEvidence, technical standards and methodology where primary public-agency guidance is not the right source.
3Qualified professional or major institution educationContext and practical explanation, checked against primary material when the topic is regulated or time-sensitive.
4Secondary articles and commentaryDiscovery and perspective, not the sole authority for a material rule or numeric limit.

How sources appear on guide pages

Each specialist guide includes an “Authoritative resources” section when primary references are directly relevant. Time-sensitive numerical values should state the applicable year. If ShareCapsule makes a planning judgment that is not an official rule, it should appear in a labeled “ShareCapsule opinion” block.

A link to an external source does not imply that the source endorses ShareCapsule Finance. Likewise, ShareCapsule’s interpretation should not be treated as the source’s official wording.

Correction policy

Prefer correction over silent ambiguity

If a source changes or a page is materially outdated, the page should be updated and its “Last reviewed” date refreshed.

Do not preserve stale numeric limits for SEO

Annual tax, retirement, insurance and similar limits should be updated or clearly labeled with their year.

Separate source facts from recommendations

Official facts should remain attributable; ShareCapsule heuristics should remain visibly labeled as our planning opinion.
