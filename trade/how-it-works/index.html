The core idea

Don't tell me what to buy. Tell me what changed and why I should care.

Ticker Watch is designed as decision support, not an automated trading recommendation engine.

Technical design

Research without handing over your portfolio.

The architecture minimizes what the market research service needs to know. When you refresh a company, the requested ticker can be sent to the public-market gateway. Your full financial vault, position sizes and holdings are not required for Ticker Watch research.

From noise to evidence

Separate the fact, the interpretation and the source.

A useful research tool should help you understand why something is being shown — not hide the reasoning behind an unexplained score.

01

Fact

What happened?

Review ticker-linked coverage and recent public filings for the company you selected.

02

Interpretation

Why may it matter?

Direction describes the tone of the news for the company. Impact describes the significance of the event.

03

Evidence

Where can I verify it?

Open the source article or filing and inspect material information before making a financial decision.

Direction is not a stock-price forecast. A possibly positive company development does not mean the share price will rise. A high-impact label means an event may deserve attention, not that a trade should be placed.

For investors

Keep the company thesis current.

Use Ticker Watch to notice material changes around companies you follow without turning every headline into a trading signal.

  • Review earnings and company developments.
  • Find recent SEC filings beside news coverage.
  • Track potentially meaningful business or regulatory catalysts.
  • Open original sources when a development deserves deeper research.

For active traders

Catch up faster before the next decision.

Use the same evidence through a faster lens: identify the stories that may matter, review significance, then decide what deserves your attention.

  • Prioritize high-impact stories.
  • Filter possible positive, negative and mixed coverage.
  • Scan filing activity beside market news.
  • Listen to a briefing when reading everything is impractical.

Research compression

Spend less time opening everything.

Ticker Watch is designed to compress the first stage of research. It helps identify what may deserve deeper reading; it does not replace the original source.

Privacy by design

Minimize collection first. Encrypt what still needs to move.

1

Local-first watchlist

Your preferred tickers are handled through the browser experience rather than requiring a readable portfolio to sit behind the research service.

2

Encrypted device sync

When optional watchlist sync is enabled, the relay stores AES-GCM ciphertext. The sync service is not intended to receive readable ticker symbols from that encrypted payload.

3

One-time pairing

Trusted-device pairing uses a short-lived capability so a sync key can be transferred to another device without turning the relay into the keeper of your readable watchlist.

4

No ad-tech dependency

The product is designed around user-paid research value rather than monetizing a detailed portfolio profile for advertising.

For the broader finance vault threat model, cryptographic controls and limitations, read the published security model or the dedicated Ticker Watch architecture.

What we intentionally do not promise

Research support without pretending uncertainty disappeared.

×

No guaranteed winning trades. Markets can react differently from the apparent tone of a development.

×

No automatic BUY / SELL instruction. The current product organizes evidence and context rather than issuing personalized trade recommendations.

×

No requirement to upload brokerage holdings. Company research can remain useful without collecting position sizes and portfolio value.

×

No replacement for primary-source review. Material information should be verified at the original source before a financial decision.
