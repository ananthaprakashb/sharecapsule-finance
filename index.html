Security center

Your privacy controls

Financial information should be treated as highly sensitive. Local-only remains the default; encrypted sync is optional.

Encrypted storage

The vault is encrypted with AES-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with a unique random salt. Only encrypted ciphertext, salt and initialization vector are persisted.

Optional zero-knowledge sync

When you enable device sync, the browser can connect only to the dedicated ShareCapsule sync relay. The relay stores encrypted vault ciphertext and cannot decrypt it without your vault passphrase.

Automatic lock

The decrypted vault is kept only in page memory and automatically locks after inactivity. You can lock it immediately from the header.

No credential collection

Never enter bank usernames, passwords, card numbers, full account numbers, Social Security numbers or authentication codes here.

Encrypted backup

Download the encrypted vault exactly as stored on this device. The same passphrase is required to restore it.

Erase this device

Permanently remove the local encrypted vault from this browser.
