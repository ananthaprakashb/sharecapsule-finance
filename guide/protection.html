Protection

Insure the losses that could permanently damage the plan.

Insurance is not mainly about avoiding every out-of-pocket expense. It is about transferring financial risks that the household cannot safely absorb.

ShareCapsule opinion

Self-insure small, affordable inconveniences when it saves money—but transfer risks that could wipe out years of savings. A deductible should be high enough to control premium without becoming unaffordable during a real claim.

Health-plan control

HealthCare.gov recommends comparing total yearly costs, including premium, deductible, copayments/coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximum. For 2026 Marketplace plans, the out-of-pocket limit cannot exceed $10,600 for an individual or $21,200 for a family, though plan-specific limits may be lower.

Know the family and individual deductible separately.
Know the in-network out-of-pocket maximum.
Confirm key doctors, hospitals and prescriptions are covered.
Keep enough liquid savings to handle the deductible or other likely cost sharing.

Annual insurance review

Update home replacement value and major property changes.
Review beneficiaries and life-insurance needs after family changes.
Check whether employer disability coverage is sufficient and portable.
Compare renewal premiums and deductibles without reducing critical protection blindly.
Maintain a home inventory for major personal property.
Review liability limits as income, home equity and investments grow.
