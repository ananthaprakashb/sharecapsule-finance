About ShareCapsule Finance

Financial control without surrendering readable financial data.

ShareCapsule Finance combines privacy-first personal planning tools with practical financial education. The planner is designed to keep readable financial information on trusted devices, while the public guide explains the reasoning behind common household-finance decisions.

What we are not

ShareCapsule Finance is not a bank, broker-dealer, registered investment adviser, tax preparer, law firm, insurance agency or credit-repair company. The public guide is educational. The planner does not know every fact that could materially affect a financial decision.

Where individualized professional advice is important, the guide says so. If independent review by a CFP professional, CPA, attorney, insurance professional or another qualified reviewer is completed for a page, that fact and the scope of review should be stated specifically on that page.
