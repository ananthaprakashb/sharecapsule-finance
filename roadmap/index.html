Product roadmap

Build the financial control system in layers.

The roadmap prioritizes privacy, understandable calculations and the highest-impact household decisions before adding more complex integrations.

Available now

Encrypted local vaultBrowser-based encrypted finance storage with local calculations and automatic lock.
Cash flow & categoriesManual transactions, CSV import, budgets and monthly category visibility.
Net worth & debtAssets, liabilities, APRs, minimums and debt-priority views.
Savings goalsTargets, contributions, emergency-fund runway and forward cash projection.
Financial Control GuideEducational guidance across spending, credit, reserves, investing, retirement, insurance, taxes and reviews.
Encrypted device syncOptional QR-based trusted-device pairing and encrypted relay architecture.

Improving now

Financial Health dashboard

Combine cash flow, liquidity, debt, retirement, concentration and protection signals into an explainable control view.

Shareable calculators

Credit-card payoff, utilization, emergency fund, savings rate and net-worth calculators that work without requiring a finance vault.

Device sync hardening

Expand testing, rate limiting, conflict handling, device revocation and independent security review.

Editorial trust system

Add reviewed-by information, source standards, methodology and explicit update dates throughout the guide.

Roadmap principle

We should not add a feature simply because another finance app has it. A feature should either improve financial control, improve privacy/security, reduce repetitive work or make an important decision easier to understand.
